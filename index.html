How I Work

01.

Discovery

Understanding the core of your brand, its values, and your audience. We start with a blank slate and an open mind.

02.

Strategy

Plotting the course. Defining the positioning and messaging framework that will drive the visual identity.

03.

Ideation

The messy part. Sketching, brainstorming, and exploring a multitude of directions without boundaries.

04.

Concept

Distilling ideas into strong, distinct visual concepts. Creating the first glimpse of the brand's potential.

05.

Refinement

Honing in on the chosen direction. Stripping away the unnecessary until only the essential remains.

06.

Typography

Selecting and customizing typefaces that give the brand its unique, uncompromising voice and tone.

07.

Color & Form

Establishing a robust, monochromatic-focused palette and structural elements that anchor the identity.

08.

Application

Testing the identity across various touchpoints—from digital interfaces to physical packaging.

09.

Delivery

Finalizing guidelines, exporting pristine assets, and handing over the keys to your new brand.

10.

Evolution

A brand is a living thing. We provide ongoing support to ensure the identity grows and adapts over time.
